## FAQ

**Who can compete?**
Teams of students from two- and four-year institutions across the Midwest.
No prior competition experience is required.

**Do I have to qualify through a state CCDC?**
Yes — teams qualify through their state event, with a Wildcard route for
second and third place finishers.
